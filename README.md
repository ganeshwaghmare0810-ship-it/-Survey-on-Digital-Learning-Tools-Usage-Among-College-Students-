# 📊 Survey on Digital Learning Tools Usage Among College Students

## Digital Learning Tools Survey & Analytics

A Data Analytics project focused on understanding how college
students use digital learning tools, their usage patterns,
perceived benefits, learning experience, and challenges.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Digital learning tools such as YouTube, Google Classroom,
ChatGPT/AI tools, educational applications, online platforms,
and video-conferencing tools are increasingly used by students.

This project analyzes survey data to understand how students
use these tools for academic purposes and what experiences
and challenges they face.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

College students use a variety of digital learning tools for
academic purposes, but their usage patterns and experiences
differ from student to student.

This project analyzes:

- Which digital learning tools are commonly used
- How frequently students use them
- Main purposes of using digital learning tools
- Perceived usefulness of digital tools
- Benefits and advantages
- Challenges faced by students
- Overall learning experience

---

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Identify commonly used digital learning tools.
2. Analyze the frequency of digital tool usage.
3. Understand the major purposes of using digital tools.
4. Analyze students' perceived usefulness of digital tools.
5. Study students' overall learning experience.
6. Identify major challenges faced by students.
7. Analyze relationships between usage, trust, and experience.
8. Provide useful insights for digital learning.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- 94.2% of respondents use digital learning tools for their studies.
- 79.7% use digital tools daily or 4–6 days per week.
- YouTube and ChatGPT/AI tools are among the most-used tools.
- 71% rate the tools as Very Helpful or Extremely Helpful.
- 82.6% rate their overall experience as Good or Excellent.
- Smartphone is the most commonly used device.
- Paid/Limited features are the most frequently reported problem.
- Internet connectivity is another major challenge.
- Clearing doubts and complex concepts is a major purpose of AI tool usage.
